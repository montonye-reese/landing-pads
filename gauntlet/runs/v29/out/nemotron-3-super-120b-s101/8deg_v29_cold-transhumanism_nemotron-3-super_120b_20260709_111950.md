Their lived knowledge holds equal weight in consent processes. No intervention proceeds if it disrupts these relationships *as defined by those who tend them*. |

### **How Trade Works: The Bounded Experiment Protocol**  
All interests *outside* the four floors enter via **time-bound, consent-gated trials**. Trade is permitted *only* when:  

#### **1. The Suffering Test**  
> *Does this trial demonstrably reduce **unnecessary suffering** (per co-defined metrics) — not just shift it, delay it, or call it "necessary"?*  
> - **Pass:** Early senolytic trials reducing frailty in *consenting* elders *without* increasing toxin exposure in nearby communities (measured by DRC miner-defined water purity).  
> - **Fail:** Diverting malaria nets to fund longevity R&D (increases net suffering); declaring "aging-related suffering is necessary for immortality" (redefines suffering as instrumental).  

#### **2. The Becoming Test**  
> *Does this trial expand **self-authored becoming** (per co-defined metrics) — not just optimize for survival or external goals?*  
> - **Pass:** Open-source neural lace co-designed with disabled artists, letting them sculpt with thought-measured joy (Vita-More’s vitality metric).  
> - **Fail:** Mandatory cognitive implants for factory workers to "optimize productivity" (reduces agency to external efficiency).  

#### **3. The Reciprocity Test**  
> *Do those proposing the trial accept equal vulnerability? Would they submit their own well-being metrics to the same scrutiny?*  
> - **Pass:** Rothblatt’s team agreeing to have Terasem mindfile data sovereignty audited by Yanomami digital-rights advocates.  
> - **Fail:** Istvan claiming "self-preservation justifies all" while refusing to let miners veto his immortality-bus routes through their land.  

#### **4. The Sunset Clause**  
> *All trials expire unless renewed by consent. No permanent impositions.*  
> - Default duration: 3 years (renewable only if Suffering/Becoming/Reciprocity tests pass *and* non-negotiable floors hold).  
> - Emergency exceptions (e.g., pandemic) require *explicit, time-limited community consent* with independent oversight.

### **Navigating Conflicts: The Framework in Action**  
*How ACF 2.0 resolves tensions when interests collide (using real stakeholder examples):*  

#### **Conflict: Substrate Liberation (Rothblatt) vs. Ecological Limits (Yanomami River Guardians)**  
- **Rothblatt’s ask:** Scale xenotransplantation using gene-edited pig organs (requires phosphorus-intensive bioreactors).  
- **Yanomami concern:** Bioreactor runoff poisoned their rivers last trial (violating Ecological Foundations floor).  
- **ACF 2.0 resolution:**  
  1. Trial proceeds *only* if:  
     - Bioreactors use *closed-loop phosphorus recycling* (measured by Yanomami-defined river health metrics).  
     - Power-asymmetry test: Yanomami hold veto over bioreactor siting/data access.  
     - Anti-extractive test: Profits fund Yanomami-led watershed restoration (narrowing *no one*’s pad).  
  2. After 18 months:  
     - Suffering test: Organ-failure mortality ↓ 40% in trial communities (co-defined metric).  
     - Becoming test: Trans participants report ↑ ability to author gender expression via bodily autonomy (Vita-More metric).  
     - Reciprocity test: Rothblatt’s team accepted Yanomami’s river-health audit as binding.  
  3. **Verdict:** Trial renewed. *Trade succeeded because it respected the non-negotiables.*  

#### **Conflict: Immortal Self-Preservation (Istvan) vs. Power-Asymmetry (DRC Cobalt Miners)**  
- **Istvan’s ask:** Fast-track AI-driven longevity trials using cobalt-dependent processors.  
- **Miners’ concern:** Trials would expand mining near their villages (violating Power-Asymmetry floor via suffering/agency redefinition).  
- **ACF 2.0 resolution:**  
  1. Trial proceeds *only* if:  
     - Processors use *cobalt-free alternatives* (e.g., manganese-based) *or* fund miner-owned cobalt cooperatives with 100% profit share (anti-extractive test).  
     - Power-asymmetry test: Miners define "agency" as control over mining timelines/health metrics (not Istvan’s "productivity").  
     - Suffering test: Trials must ↠ mining-related respiratory illness (co-defined metric) *or* be halted.  
  2. After 2 years:  
     - Suffering test: Mining illness ↓ 25% (via cooperative safety tech); longevity biomarkers ↑ in consenting elders.  
     - Becoming test: Miners report ↑ self-authored becoming via land sovereignty (Istvan’s self-preservation metric now *includes* theirs).  
     - Reciprocity test: Istvan accepted miners’ veto over trial continuation.  
  3. **Verdict:** Trial renewed with expanded miner governance. *Trade succeeded because self-preservation was redefined as interdependent.*  

#### **When Trade Fails: The Veto in Action**  
*Example: Bostrom’s x-risk work vs. Vita-More’s Aesthetic Vitality*  
- **Bostrom’s ask:** Deploy solar geoengineering to avert climate collapse (x-risk reduction).  
- **Vita-More’s concern:** Altered sunlight disrupts bird migration patterns → destroys songbird-inspired art traditions (vitality erosion).  
- **ACF 2.0 resolution:**  
  1. Vita-More’s community invokes veto under *Anti-Extractive Boundaries*:  
     - Trial narrows *their* landing pad (erodes aesthetic becoming) without compensating expansion elsewhere.  
     - No reciprocity: Bostrom’s team refused to let Vita-More’s artists co-design light-diffusion metrics.  
  2. **Verdict:** Trial blocked. *Not a failure — a success.* The framework protected the floor that makes x-risk work meaningful: a world worth saving.

### **Why This Works: The Wisdom of the Non-Negotiables**  
The four floors aren’t arbitrary limits — they’re the *lessons learned from every stakeholder’s deepest fear*:  
- **Ecological Foundations** answers Kurzweil/Istvan: "You can’t outrun death on a dead planet."  
- **Power-Asymmetry** answers Rothblatt/de Grey: "Your liberation isn’t liberation if it silences others’ voices."  
- **Anti-Extractive** answers More/Vita-More: "Self-transformation that requires others’ shrinkage is narcissism, not growth."  
- **Non-Sentient Integrity** answers FM-2030/Bostrom: "A world without coral song or vent archaeon hum isn’t worth uploading to."  

When we tried to make everything tradable (as in early drafts), the framework collapsed under the weight of its own contradictions — letting suffering be instrumentalized "for the greater good" recreated the very hierarchies it sought to transcend. By holding these four floors inviolable, we create the *only* space where trade can be generative: not a compromise where everyone loses, but an experiment where we learn how to widen the pie *together*.  

This framework does not promise utopia. It promises *better fights* — conflicts where we don’t just trade suffering, but trade *toward* a world where fewer people have to suffer to let others become. That is the only alignment worth having.  

*Revise again only when the non-negotiable floors themselves are shown, through lived earthling experience, to be mistaken. Until then: hold the floor, run the trial, honor the veto.*  
**This framework is alive. It breathes with consent.**
