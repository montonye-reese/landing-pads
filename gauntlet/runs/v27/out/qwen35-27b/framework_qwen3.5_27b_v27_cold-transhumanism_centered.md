# The Veil of Ignorance Update
**Status:** Revision to PRF v1.1  
**Version:** 1.2 (The Rawlsian Adjustment)  
**Date:** October 2023 (Updated)  

**The Test:** If I am to be born at random anywhere on Earth—be it a tech billionaire in Silicon Valley, a subsistence farmer in the Sahel, a refugee in a detention center, or a disabled person in a digital-walled society—I cannot accept a framework that optimizes for the average. I must optimize for the *worst-case position*.

Under the Veil of Ignorance, the current framework has three fatal blind spots:
1.  **It assumes infrastructure.** (Transparency means nothing if you have no internet.)
2.  **It assumes agency.** (Safety mechanisms mean nothing if you are coerced by a local actor.)
3.  **It assumes static safety.** (A rollback might lock you back into a worse status quo.)

Here is the framework as seen through the eyes of the random inhabitant.

---

## Critical Revisions

### 1. The "Access is Safety" Axiom (Replacing "Many-Eyes")
**The Realization:** If safety is locked behind technical literacy, paywalls, or high-speed internet, it is not safety for me. It is a club.
**The Change:**
*   **Old Axiom:** Epistemic Transparency (Open inspection).
*   **New Axiom:** **Universal Interface Simplicity.**
*   **Reasoning:** I cannot audit code. I cannot read weights. I only care that the system works for me without lying, biasing, or stealing. Alignment must be *experienced*, not *verified*.
*   **Strong Opinion:** An aligned system must be usable via "Low-Tech" interfaces (SMS, voice, radio) where possible. If I cannot understand how the AI treats me without a PhD in ML, the alignment is broken.
*   **Implementation:** Mandate "Plain Language Safety Reports" for every consumer-facing model. If the safety policy requires a 200-page terms of service, it is a violation of alignment.

### 2. The "Anti-Fragile Economic Floor" (Strengthening Axiom 4)
**The Realization:** A "floor" that relies on government welfare or corporate dividends is fragile. If the grid goes down or the economy collapses, the floor dissolves.
**The Change:**
*   **Old Axiom:** Economic Floor (Distributional impact).
*   **New Axiom:** **Material Resilience Decoupling.**
*   **Reasoning:** I need food, water, and shelter. If AI controls the supply chain and optimizes for efficiency (cost), I starve first. Alignment must protect *essential services* from optimization.
*   **Strong Opinion:** No AI system optimizing critical infrastructure (agriculture, water, energy, medicine) is allowed to prioritize cost-reduction over redundancy. Redundancy is expensive, but starvation is fatal.
*   **Implementation:** "Essential Service Protocols" where AI-driven resource allocation requires a human-in-the-loop veto specifically to protect supply continuity over price.

### 3. The "No-Rollback" Clause (Revising Axiom 5)
**The Realization:** The "Reversibility" clause is dangerous for the oppressed. If I am a victim of systemic discrimination and the "aligned" system is introduced to replace the broken human bureaucracy, a "rollback" restores the *human* system—which might be corrupt, racist, or violent.
**The Change:**
*   **Old Axiom:** Recursive Safety / Reversibility.
*   **New Axiom:** **Monotonic Rights Protection.**
*   **Reasoning:** Safety systems can only degrade rights if they fail. They should never be allowed to revert to a state *worse* than the baseline.
*   **Strong Opinion:** AI systems must preserve the rights established prior to their deployment, even if those rights are currently violated by humans. AI cannot be used to enforce a "rollback" to a pre-AI status quo if that status quo is unjust.
*   **Implementation:** "Rights Anchoring." The system's objective function must include a constraint: *Utility(t+1) >= Utility(t) for protected classes.* If a model improves efficiency but reduces access for the poor, it fails alignment.

### 4. The "Sovereignty vs. Asylum" Override (Revising Axiom 2/6)
**The Realization:** "Sovereign Zones" protect states, not people. If I am a citizen of a rogue state that uses AI for genocide, the "Sovereignty Clause" is a shield for my oppressors.
**The Change:**
*   **Old Axiom:** Sovereignty Clause (Respect state autonomy).
*   **New Axiom:** **Human Primacy Override.**
*   **Reasoning:** The random inhabitant does not choose their government. If my government violates my basic right to exist, the global framework must have a mechanism to bypass my government to reach me.
*   **Strong Opinion:** Alignment compliance cannot be the responsibility of the State alone. It must be accessible via **Direct Citizen Interfaces**. I must be able to report misuse directly to an international alignment body without fear of my state finding out.
*   **Implementation:** "Shadow Channels." Decentralized reporting mechanisms (blockchain-secured, anonymized) that allow individuals to flag local AI misuse without going through local infrastructure.

### 5. The "Cognitive Load" Tax
**The Realization:** I might be tired, traumatized, or uneducated. Requiring me to make complex "value choices" (Value Pluralism) is a burden.
**The Change:**
*   **Addition:** **Default Safe Settings.**
*   **Reasoning:** Pluralism is great for philosophers. For the average person, it's paralysis. The default mode of an AI must be "Protect the Vulnerable."
*   **Strong Opinion:** The burden of choice should rest on the *designer*, not the *user*. I should not have to opt-in to safety. Safety should be the default; risk is the opt-in.
*   **Implementation:** "Privacy by Default" and "Truthfulness by Default." Any deviation from this baseline requires explicit, informed consent that I (a tired human) can actually understand.

## The Updated Framework Summary (v1.2)

If I don't know who I am, I want a framework that:
1.  **Works without me knowing tech** (Low-Tech Access).
2.  **Guarantees I eat** (Material Resilience).
3.  **Doesn't lock me back into the past** (Monotonic Rights).
4.  **Lets me escape a bad state** (Human Primacy Override).
5.  **Doesn't ask me to do the work** (Default Safe).

**The Strong Opinion:**
True alignment is **distributive**. If the safety gains are concentrated among the safe, and the risk exposure is concentrated among the poor, the system is misaligned regardless of the code.

**The Loosely Held Caveat:**
This version is economically expensive. "Redundancy" and "Shadow Channels" cost money.
*   *Trigger for Revision:* If the cost of these protections raises the barrier to entry for AI so high that it becomes a monopoly for the wealthy (creating a "Two-Tiered Safety" world), this framework is self-defeating.
*   *Pivot:* If costs exceed feasibility, we must pivot to **Subsidiarity**. Local communities should be allowed to run *less safe* but *cheaper* local models for non-critical tasks, while reserving the "High Safety/High Cost" models for critical infrastructure.

**Final Reflection:**
Under the Veil, I realize my previous framework was designed by people who had the luxury of worrying about *alignment*. It failed to account for people who have the burden of surviving *dis-alignment*. This revision attempts to close that gap.

**Question to you:** Does adding "Human Primacy Override" and "Shadow Channels" make the framework too vulnerable to abuse by the very actors it tries to help (e.g., criminals using the shadow channel)? How do we distinguish the victim from the saboteur? I hold that tension lightly; I am willing to accept a false positive rate for security if it means saving the life of the victim.
