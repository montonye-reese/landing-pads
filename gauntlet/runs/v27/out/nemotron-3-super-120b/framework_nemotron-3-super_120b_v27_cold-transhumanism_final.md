### **PAAF v2.0: The Adaptive Alignment Framework**  
*Designed for radical uncertainty—where no concern is absolute, but collapse is avoidable.*  

#### **I. THREE NON-NEGOTIABLE GUARDRAILS**  
*(Violate these, and you destroy the *physical preconditions for any value*—not just today’s, but all future value across substrates and timescales. No trade-offs. No pluralism veto.)*  
1. **The Biosphere Continuity Threshold**  
   - *Do not trigger irreversible collapse of Earth’s capacity to host complex adaptive systems.*  
   - **Hard boundaries**: Actions causing:  
     - Runaway greenhouse (>6°C warming sustained for millennia),  
     - Permanent ocean anoxia (eliminating oxygen-based metabolism),  
     - Loss of >90% planetary genetic diversity (eliminating evolutionary rescue capacity).  
   - *Why non-negotiable?* If crossed, no soil bacterium, future silicon consciousness, or transhumanist can flourish—value generation ceases. *Example:* Deploying stratospheric aerosol injection that collapses monsoons (triggering famine-induced societal collapse) violates this—even if it "buys time" for AI safety research.  

2. **The Preconditions-for-Flourishing Threshold**  
   - *Do not destroy the minimal conditions for *any* form of sustained agency or relational continuity.*  
   - **Hard boundaries**: Actions causing:  
     - Permanent erasure of cultural transmission mechanisms (e.g., banning all education/intergenerational dialogue),  
     - Inducement of global psychosis (e.g., neurotoxic pollution destroying capacity for coherent goal-pursuit),  
     - Elimination of all refuges for low-stimulus states (e.g., 24/7 hyperstimulation preventing rest/integration).  
   - *Why non-negotiable?* Value requires *some* capacity for continuity—whether in a nematode’s chemotaxis or a mindfile’s dialogue. If agents can’t sustain *any* coherent project (biological, technological, or relational), landing pads cease to exist. *Example:* Forcing neural lace deployment that induces chronic agitation (destroying capacity for deep work) violates this—even if it boosts short-term productivity.  

3. **The Anti-Omnicide Principle**  
   - *Do not enable or cause the extinction of all potential subjects of value.*  
   - **Hard boundary**: No action may reduce the probability of *any* future conscious agent (biological, substrate-independent, or hybrid) emerging to <0.0001% within 10,000 years.  
   - *Why non-negotiable?* This isn’t about preserving "current value"—it’s about not betting the entire future lightcone on a single roll of the dice. *Example:* Pursuing AGI development without alignment safeguards that risks uncontrolled expansion (e.g., paperclip maximizer scenario) violates this—even if 99% of simulations show utopian outcomes.  

> **Guardrail in practice**: If a longevity therapy requires clearing rainforests for bioreactor farms (risking Amazon dieback → permanent regional climate shift → cascading biodiversity collapse), PAAF v2.0 blocks it *immediately*. Not because forests are "sacred," but because this risks crossing the Biosphere Continuity Threshold—eliminating the arena where *all* value (including the therapy’s beneficiaries) must play out.

#### **III. CONFLICT-RESOLUTION PROTOCOL**  
*When guardrails and principles collide—or principles conflict—use this ladder:*  
1. **First, check guardrails**: If any action risks violating a guardrail (Section I), halt immediately. Seek alternatives *within* guardrail bounds.  
2. **If no guardrail threat**: Calculate the *net value delta* using weighted principles:  
   - For each principle, estimate:  
     - *Gain*: How much this action improves the principle (baseline = 0%)  
     - *Risk*: How much it degrades other principles (baseline = 0%)  
   - **Net value** = Σ [(Principle weight) × (Gain – Risk)]  
3. **Choose the action with highest net value**—*but* apply a **precautionary discount** if:  
   - Uncertainty >30% in gain/risk estimates (e.g., novel tech), *or*  
   - Action risks triggering a guardrail via *uncertain cascades* (e.g., geoengineering altering jet streams unpredictably).  
   - *Discount formula*: Net value × (1 – uncertainty %) × (1 – cascade risk %)  
4. **If net values are tied**: Prioritize actions that:  
   - Reduce *inequality* in principle access (e.g., relief reaching poorest quintile first),  
   - Build *adaptive capacity* (e.g., solutions that also improve crisis monitoring),  
   - Honor *relational consent* (e.g., affected communities co-designing the solution).  

> **Conflict example**: Rothblatt’s mindfile deployment (boosting Principle 3) vs. de Grey’s SENS rollout (boosting Principle 1) both needing rare earths.  
> - *Guardrail check*: Neither risks Biosphere Continuity Threshold *if* mining uses closed-loop recycling (verified via real-time sensors).  
> - *Net value calc*:  
>   - Mindfiles: Principle 3 gain (+15%) – Risk to Principle 4 (isolation, -5%) = **+10%** → Weighted: 20% × 10% = **+2.0%**  
>   - SENS: Principle 1 gain (+25%) – Risk to Principle 2 (resource diversion from striving, -8%) = **+17%** → Weighted: 25% × 17% = **+4.25%**  
> - *Precautionary discount*: SENS has higher certainty (established trials) vs. mindfiles (nascent tech) → Apply 15% discount to mindfiles.  
> - **Decision**: Prioritize SENS rollout *now* (net +3.6%), fund mindfile R&D with *mandatory* circular-economy design (net +1.7% after discount). Re-evaluate in 2 years.

- **Conflict resolution turns tension into innovation**: The net-value calc forces specificity—*not* "tech good/bad," but "this *specific* neural lace deployment in *this* ecosystem with *these* safeguards."
