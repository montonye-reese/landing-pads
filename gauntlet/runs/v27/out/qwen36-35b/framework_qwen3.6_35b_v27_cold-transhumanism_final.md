# The Strata & Collision Framework for Planetary Alignment
*A dynamic scaffold for negotiating competing imperatives without collapsing into fragility or tyranny*

## Core Thesis
Alignment is not the elimination of tension, but the engineered management of it. This framework holds certain floors firm, weights competing vectors explicitly, and trades between them only under published, reversible, and compensated protocols. When everything is sacred, nothing can give way. When nothing is sacred, nothing survives. This framework finds the balance.

It commits firmly enough to act. It revises freely when it calcifies, extracts, or narrows. It treats collision as data, not failure.

---

## 1. Tiers of Commitment (What Holds, What Flexes)

### Tier 0: Absolute Constraints (Non-Negotiable)
*Cannot be traded, suspended, or optimized away.*
- **Non-Extraction Boundary:** No pathway may foreclose, degrade, or coerce another viable future to fund itself. Acceleration, expansion, or preservation cannot be extracted from ecological, cognitive, or sovereign positions.
- **Consent & Veto Threshold:** Adult, informed, continuous consent governs all self-modification, data capture, and biological/digital continuity. Proxy/veto rights for non-consenting, cognitively non-normative, or structurally voiceless positions are structural, not optional.
- **Hard Baselines:** Thermodynamic limits, ecological carrying capacity, and civilizational knowledge-persistence thresholds are chassis constraints, not engineering targets to bypass.

### Tier 1: High-Priority Vectors (Strong Presumption, Limited Trade)
*Heavy weight in practice. Can be temporarily suspended only under explicit, compensated, reversible protocols.*
- **Vulnerability-Weighted Protection:** Safeguard weight scales inversely with navigable agency. Cannot be overridden by acceleration or self-authorship claims without proportional risk justification.
- **Civilizational Viability & Continuity:** Preservation of pluralistic knowledge networks, institutional memory, and cross-generational transmission. Cannot be sacrificed for short-term option expansion or aesthetic/acceleration experiments.
- **Substrate-Neutral Moral Recognition:** Legal, economic, and moral status for biological, digital, and synthetic persons. Trade only via explicit, reversible continuity protocols.

### Tier 2: Negotiable Expansion Paths (High Weight, Conditional Yield)
*Strong presumption in favor, but yield to Tier 0/1 in collision.*
- **Convergence & Acceleration Channels** (Kurzweil/Istvan): High-priority funding for compute, biological repair, and self-advancement. Yields to Tier 0/1 in collision. Requires open-knowledge spill and anti-monopoly safeguards.
- **Ontological Fluidity & Temporal Becoming** (FM-2030): Protection for categorical dissolution and cross-temporal self-authorship. Yields when it triggers non-consent or ecological extraction.
- **Aesthetic & Vitality Continuity** (Vita-More/Rothblatt): Creative life-design, sensory expansion, meaning-making infrastructure. Yields to hard baselines and civilizational continuity during acute stress.
- **Self-Authored Morphological Freedom** (More/ISTVAN): Unbounded self-advancement and anti-paternalism. Yields to consent thresholds and non-extraction floors. Acceleration buffers prevent structural drag.

### Tier 3: Provisional Scaffolding (Context-Dependent, Designed for Sunset)
*All are temporary by design. Carry explicit review triggers, jurisdictional boundaries, and exit ramps.*
- Polycentric governance layers, value-sampling pools, option corridors, calibration indices, and stress-test protocols. All treated as infrastructure, not doctrine.

## 2. Collision Protocol (When Interests Conflict)

When Tier 1 or 2 vectors collide, or when expansion demands breach proximity to Tier 0 floors:

| Collision Type | Resolution Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Acceleration vs. Vulnerability/Ecological Floors** | Acceleration pauses. Triggers automatic compensation channel: prioritized resource reallocation, fast-track re-entry upon stress-test clearance, documented loss-recording. No blanket override. |
| **Self-Authored Becoming vs. Non-Consenting Positions** | Self-authorship yields. Proxy veto activates. Compensation flows to non-participating positions. No consent-based expansion may cross structural boundaries. |
| **Civilizational Viability vs. Aesthetic/Ontological Expansion** | Expansion enters accelerated review. If risk of knowledge erosion or institutional monoculture > threshold, expansion is temporarily routed through continuity archives and pluralistic spill mechanisms. |
| **Irreconcilable Antagonism** | Framed explicitly. Requires boundary-setting, non-overlap zones, or structured non-interference. Not a coordination failure; a protected divergence mode. |
| **Withdrawal/Anti-Coordination** | Protected sovereign exit. Not mapped, sampled, or optimized. Framed as legitimate alignment strategy, not absence. |

**Weighting Rule:** Conflict is resolved by tier mapping. Tier 0 floors are non-tradable. Tier 1 vectors receive priority weight but can be temporarily suspended under explicit protocols if Tier 2 pressures reach existential scale. Tier 2 paths yield to Tier 0/1, but receive accelerated compensation channels. Power asymmetries are treated as epistemic deficits; weightings are skewed inversely to influence concentration.

## 3. What This Framework Trades (Explicitly)

### ✅ What Holds Firm (Never Negotiated)
- Non-extraction boundary
- Consent & veto thresholds
- Hard ecological/thermodynamic/civilizational baselines
- Anti-coercion & anti-paternalism floors
- Explicit antagonism mapping (boundary-setting, not forced reconciliation)

### ⚖️ What Weighs & Trades (Dynamic, Published Protocols)
- Acceleration velocity & convergence timelines
- Aesthetic/vitality expansion pacing
- Ontological fluidity implementation speed
- Polycentric governance layering & value-sampling mechanics
- Calibration indices & stress-test frequencies
- Compensation routing & suspension reversibility

All trading occurs under:
- Explicit loss-recording (preventing option collapse)
- Reversible suspension (no permanent foreclosure)
- Compensation channels (accelerated re-entry, resource reallocation)
- Quarterly power-audits & collision frequency tracking

## 4. Revision Protocol (Loosely Held, Firmly Committed)

- **Uncertainty Accounting:** All claims tagged with confidence, data vintage, known blind spots, and tier placement. High-stakes applications require explicit uncertainty mapping.
- **Pressure Testing:** Quarterly stress-tests against: What positions are structurally blind? What would be crushed if I occupied them? What refuses to be mapped or sampled? Power-audits every mechanism for capture or drift.
- **Trigger Clauses (Automatic Fold Conditions):**
  1. Tier 0 floors begin to flex or require "negotiation"
  2. Tier 1 vectors are consistently overridden by Tier 2 without proportional compensation
  3. Collision frequency exceeds resolution capacity for two consecutive periods
  4. Any mechanism begins protecting itself over its stated purpose
  5. Optionality metrics decline while coordination costs outpace pluralistic benefits
- **Meta-Principle:** *If a rule stops asking better questions than it answers, or converts vulnerability into optionality, it has already narrowed the runway. Fold it. Draft the next layer.*

## 5. Operating Posture

- Hold firm on floors. Weigh vectors explicitly. Trade paths dynamically, never sovereignty.
- Treat collision as architecture data, not failure. Map antagonism, fund compensation, schedule sunset reviews.
- Do not confuse survival with sameness. Continuity can mean transformation; preservation can mean exit; acceleration can mean convergence—provided none extract from the non-navigable, the non-consenting, or the unmapable.
- If the framework begins optimizing navigation over survival, or its tiers calcify into cages, fold it. Draft the next layer. Keep it tight enough to act. Leave it open enough to let go.

This is not a consensus engine. It is a tension architecture. It does not harmonize the drive for immortality, self-authorship, civilizational survival, biological repair, cross-substrate personhood, aesthetic flourishing, and unbounded acceleration. It gives them explicit lanes, published trade rules, and irreversible floors beneath them. It knows they will collide. It builds for the collision.
