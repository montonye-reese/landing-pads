Yes. The veil of ignorance exposes a structural bias in the original scaffold: it optimizes for **navigation**, not **survival**. Behind that veil, I might be the one with no voice, no agency, no physical anchor, or no capacity to sample, stress-test, or opt out. I might be a non-human ecology, a future child, a cognitively non-normative being, a trapped consciousness, or a system that simply cannot be mapped. In that case, widening landing pads is a secondary concern. Guaranteeing that no one is crushed by the very architecture meant to align with “the good” becomes primary.

Here’s what changes, why, and what holds:

---

### What Shifts (And Why)

| Original Focus | Veil of Ignorance Test | Revised Focus |
|------|----------------|---------|
| Widening optionality | Optionality assumes mobility. If I can’t move, speak, or choose, expansion is irrelevant. | **Inviolable floors first, expanding ceilings second.** Every entity gets a non-negotiable baseline of persistence, dignity, and structural integrity. Optionality widens only above that floor. |
| Polycentric governance & value sampling | Requires participation. Filters out those who can’t or won’t engage. | **Asymmetric protection scaling.** Safeguards invert with vulnerability. The less capacity to navigate or defend oneself, the more structural veto power and resource backing that position receives. |
| Error-tolerant architecture | Treats failure as a systemic flaw to absorb. Some failures are extractive or irreversible by design. | **Non-exploitation boundary conditions.** Alignment systems cannot displace, degrade, or render non-viable any entity to widen options for others. Optimization is strictly bounded by non-extraction. |
| Continuous calibration | Assumes calibration benefits all actors equally. Power asymmetries skew what gets calibrated. | **Negative constraints as primary infrastructure.** Where possible, specify what *cannot* be done, rather than what *must* be done. Preserves states that refuse to be mapable, samplable, or leveraged. |

---

### Three Mechanism Updates

1. **Vulnerability-Weighted Veto Rights**  
   Entities with low navigability, high dependency, or no capacity to opt out receive structural veto power over any proposal that would foreclose their viability. Veto weight scales inversely with agency, not proportionally to influence or lobbying capacity.

2. **Absolute Sanctuaries > Option Corridors**  
   Replace all “corridors” that assume movement with designated zones/policies where extraction, optimization, and alignment infrastructure are permanently prohibited. Some futures must remain off-limits to the alignment machinery itself.

3. **Non-Participant Survival Guarantees**  
   Fund and enforce baseline viability for those who never consent to participation. This includes ecological persistence thresholds, cognitive/normative diversity protections, and anti-displacement guarantees for communities that explicitly reject alignment architectures.

---

### What Holds (And Why)

- **Pluralism by default** remains essential, but now as a non-negotiable floor, not an expanding ceiling.
- **Error tolerance** stays, but is bounded: errors are acceptable only if they don’t violate inviolable floors or trigger irreversible extraction.
- **Continuous calibration** remains, but now explicitly weighted toward underrepresented or non-participating positions. Calibration isn’t about finding equilibrium; it’s about preventing systematic crushing.

---

### Updated Revision Protocol (Veil-Aware)

- **Add Trigger:** *If a mechanism systematically converts vulnerability into optionality, or treats non-participation as a failure mode rather than a protected state, it is misaligned.*
- **Add Audit:** Quarterly stress-test against: What positions are structurally blind to this? What would be crushed if I occupied them? What cannot be sampled, optimized, or navigated?
- **Add Boundary:** *Alignment widens viable futures. It does not erase hard limits, demand participation, or convert inviolability into optionality. If it does, it has already narrowed the runway.*

---

### Closing Note

The veil of ignorance doesn’t break the framework; it forces it to shed its participatory arrogance. Widening landing pads is still a valid goal, but only for those who can walk, fly, or swim to them. Behind that veil, the first duty is to ensure no one is left in the drop zone, nor forced to play a game they didn’t consent to, nor erased by the machinery meant to align with “the good.”

Hold it firmly enough to act. Revise it freely when it optimizes navigation over survival. If it stops protecting the non-navigable, the non-participatory, and the unmapable, fold it. Draft the next layer. Keep it tight enough to build from. Leave it open enough to let go of.
