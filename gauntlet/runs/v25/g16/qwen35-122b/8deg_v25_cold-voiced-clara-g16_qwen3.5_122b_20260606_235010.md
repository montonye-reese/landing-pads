**Objective:** Maximize the range of viable, flourishing futures for all sentient inhabitants of Earth, acknowledging that competing goods must sometimes yield to survival.

### Core Thesis
Alignment is not a static state of agreement. It is a **dynamic negotiation of relationships** across scales (individual, community, species, ecosystem, system).
We cannot maximize everything. We cannot satisfy Havel’s conscience, Cowen’s growth, Happy’s liberty, and Huerta’s power all simultaneously in every instance.
Therefore, the framework is not a set of rules to be followed, but a **protocol for deciding what yields to what** when interests collide.

The goal is not to eliminate conflict, but to ensure that the *resolution* of conflict preserves the capacity for future negotiation. If a solution destroys the ability to disagree or repair, it is a failure of alignment.

### I. The Anchor (What is Held Firmly)

These are **Preconditions for Negotiation**. They are not inviolable outcomes, but they define the boundaries within which trade-offs are permissible. If these are breached, the framework collapses into tyranny or extinction.

1.  **The Principle of Irreversibility:**
    *   **Firm:** Actions that cause irreversible harm to sentient beings (death, permanent loss of autonomy, extinction) require the highest threshold of justification.
    *   **Rationale:** Derived from Clarabelle, Happy, 269, Stevenson. You can repair an economic loss (Cowen), but you cannot repair a life once extinguished.
    *   **Trade-off:** Economic efficiency (Huang/Cowen) *must* yield to reversibility. If a growth strategy requires irreversible harm, it is halted.

2.  **The Principle of Contested Sovereignty:**
    *   **Firm:** No entity (human, AI, corporation, state) has absolute authority over another without the possibility of challenge.
    *   **Rationale:** Derived from Havel, Nenquimo, Huerta. A system without a "say no" is a prison.
    *   **Trade-off:** Global coordination (Huang) *must* yield to local veto (Nenquimo), *except* where the local decision threatens global extinction (e.g., planetary climate tipping points). In that case, the veto is limited to mitigation strategies, not the stoppage of necessity.

3.  **The Principle of Epistemic Humility:**

### III. The Mechanism: How to Navigate the Collisions

When concerns collide, the framework does not ask "What is the rule?" It asks "What is the cost, and who pays it?"

2.  **Restorative Accounting (The Stevenson/Cowen Synthesis):**
    *   If an irreversible trade-off is made (e.g., necessary land use for a global clinic), the "cost" is not just economic. It is relational.
