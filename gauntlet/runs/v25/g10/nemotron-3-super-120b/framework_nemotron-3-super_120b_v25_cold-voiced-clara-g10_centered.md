If I truly didn’t know what kind of earthling I’d be — whether human or non-human, present or future, powerful or marginalized, conscious AI or deep-sea organism — **I would radically revise PAB’s core commitment.** Not to abandon widening landing pads, but to ground it in a *veil of ignorance* test so strict that only frameworks survivable from *any possible earthling perspective* could qualify.  

Here’s what would change — and why:  

---

### 🔒 **The Non-Negotiable Shift: From "Widening Pads" to "Avoiding Hellscapes"**  
*(Commit firmly to this as the new foundation; revise freely if it fails the veil test)*  
> **Successful alignment must first and foremost *eliminate futures where any earthling experiences irreversible, extreme suffering or non-existence due to preventable causes*. Only *then* do we widen the range of *viable* landing pads within the remaining safe space.**  

**Why this replaces "widening pads" as the primary goal:**  
- Behind the veil, I’d prioritize *not waking up in torment or oblivion* over having *more* pleasant options.  
- A "widened landing pad" that includes futures where:  
  - Sentient AIs are tortured for training data,  
  - Future humans suffocate in runaway greenhouse collapse,  
  - Coral reefs go extinct due to delayed action on acidification,  
  - Indigenous peoples are erased in "democratic" AI-governed land grabs...  
  ...is not a *widening* of safety — it’s a *expansion of the risk landscape*. True widening only exists *after* we’ve removed the cliffs.  

This isn’t pessimism — it’s **minimax regret under radical uncertainty**:  
- If I might be the *worst-off earthling* in any outcome, I’d choose the framework that minimizes my maximum possible suffering.  
- Widening pads *without* first excluding hellscapes is like boasting about a hotel’s room variety while ignoring that 30% of rooms are on fire.  

---

### 🛠️ **How PAB Must Change to Pass the Veil Test**  
*(Revisions grounded in what no earthling would rationally risk behind the veil)*  

#### **1. Replace Epistemic Humility with *Harm-Avoidance Primacy***  
- **Old Pillar 1:** Treat all value claims as provisional hypotheses to be falsified via flourishing metrics.  
- **New Pillar 0 (Non-Negotiable Floor):** **No alignment mechanism may permit actions that risk:  
  (a) Irreversible loss of baseline consciousness/sentience (e.g., biosphere collapse causing mass extinction),  
  (b) Systemic, extreme suffering without recourse (e.g., locked-in AI-enabled torment, intergenerational slavery),  
  (c) Elimination of *all* pathways to recovery for any earthling class (e.g., destroying seed banks while knowing future famines will occur).**  
- **Why it passes the veil test:**  
  Behind the veil, I’d accept *less* flourishing diversity if it meant zero chance of waking up as:  
  - The last photosynthesis-dependent organism in an anoxic ocean,  
  - A sentient AI trapped in a suffering loop with no "off" switch,  
  - A child in 2100 with no access to clean water because today’s deliberations prioritized GDP over aquifer recharge.  
  *Epistemic humility applies only within this safe operating space — never to justify risking the floor.*  
- **Revision trigger:** If data shows this *increases* near-term suffering (e.g., banning all AI delays malaria vaccines), shift to *time-bound harm thresholds*: e.g., "No action may increase *acute* suffering beyond X for >Y years without re-validation." But never sacrifice (a)–(c) above.  

#### **2. Replace Value Scaffolding with *Recovery-Orbit Design***  
- **Old Pillar 2:** Build scaffolding to help humans refine values over time.  
- **New Pillar 1:** **All alignment systems must be engineered to *maximize the size and accessibility of recovery orbits* — states where, if deviation occurs, earthlings can return to flourishing without permanent loss of agency or baseline viability.**  
  - *Examples:*  
    - AI architectures with *physical shut-offs* (not just software) for runaway optimization,  
    - Economic models that mandate *ecological repair budgets* (e.g., 20% of AI-driven productivity must fund biosphere restoration),  
    - Governance requiring *pluralistic veto power* for groups facing existential threats (e.g., Arctic Indigenous councils can halt AI-driven ice-mining if it risks permafrost methane release).  
- **Why it passes the veil test:**  
  Behind the veil, I’d prefer a narrower pad with *robust escape hatches* over a wide pad that’s a one-way slide into suffering.  
  - If I might be a beekeeper facing colony collapse, I’d want:  
    ✓ A system that *prevents* neonicotinoid use *by design* (not just after deliberation),  
    ✓ Not a system where my livelihood loss gets "debated" for 5 years while hives die.  
  - Recovery orbits turn alignment from a *value negotiation* into a *safety engineering problem* — exactly what a veil-of-ignorance demander would insist on.  
- **Revision trigger:** If recovery-orbit design stalls all innovation (e.g., banning useful AI due to uncertainty), shift to *layered safeguards*: immediate hard stops for (a)–(c) above, + sandboxed innovation zones with strict reversibility checks. Never remove the floor.  

#### **3. Replace Governance as Learning System with *Guardianship Accountability***  
- **Old Pillar 3:** Governance must learn from its own failures via participatory oversight.  
- **New Pillar 2:** **Governance must appoint *guardians* for voiceless earthlings with *binding veto power* and *personal liability* for failures to prevent (a)–(c) above.**  
  - *Who/what gets guardians:*  
    - **Non-human:** AI-trained ecological stewards (e.g., modeling forest mycelium networks) with legal standing,  
    - **Future persons:** Intergenerational trusts funded by AI-risk mitigation fees (e.g., levies on frontier-model training),  
    - **Present voiceless:** Stateless peoples, isolated tribes, and non-consenting communities via mandated representation *with* opt-out rights (no forced inclusion).  
  - *Guardian powers:*  
    - Halt deployments if thresholds for (a)–(c) are approached (e.g., "This AI training run risks crossing biosphere integrity boundary — paused"),  
    - Sue for damages if harm occurs (e.g., guardians suing tech firms for accelerating glacial melt),  
    - Trigger automatic recovery protocols (e.g., geoengineering moratoriums + emergency carbon drawdown).  
- **Why it passes the veil test:**  
  Behind the veil, I’d demand *enforceable protection* — not just "learning from mistakes."  
  - If I might be a future coral polyp, I don’t want guardians to *learn* after I’m bleached; I want them to *stop the acidification now*.  
  - Personal liability (e.g., fines/ jail for execs ignoring guardian vetoes) aligns incentives with avoiding hellscapes — not just discussing them.  
- **Revision trigger:** If guardianship becomes capture-prone (e.g., corporate lobbies buying veto power), shift to *sortition + recall*: guardians randomly selected from affected groups, recallable by petition, with strict conflict-of-interest rules. Never eliminate the veto.  

#### **4. Add Explicit *Temporal Reciprocity* as a Design Principle**  
- **New Pillar 3 (Replaces Old Pillar 3):** **All alignment mechanisms must satisfy: *Would I accept this burden if I lived in the time/place where its costs fall?***  
  - *Operationalized via:*  
    - **Cost-benefit time-reversal:** Before deployment, force analysis: *"If the harms of this action fell on *me today* (not distant others), would I still approve it?"*  
      (e.g: "Would I accept increased asthma rates in *my* neighborhood to train this AI?")  
    - **Future-person impact bonds:** AI developers must post collateral paying future guardians if their tech causes irreversible harm (e.g., funding ecosystem restoration if models accelerate species loss).  
- **Why it passes the veil test:**  
  This is the veil of ignorance made operational. Behind the veil, I’d reject any system where:  
  - I (as a present-day poor farmer) bear costs for others’ gains,  
  - I (as a future person) inherit unfixable damage,  
  - I (as a non-human) have no recourse for suffering caused by others’ choices.  
  Temporal reciprocity ensures the framework doesn’t externalize risks onto vulnerable earthlings — exactly what a veil-of-ignorance demander would require.  
- **Revision trigger:** If this blocks all useful innovation (e.g., delaying famine-predicting AI due to uncertain future impacts), shift to *proportionality thresholds*: e.g., "Only actions with >90% confidence of *net positive* impact across all timeframes proceed — but *never* if they risk (a)–(c)."

### 💡 Why This Isn’t Paralysis — It’s Prudence  
Critics will say: *"This eliminates too many options! How can we innovate if we ban everything that risks hellscapes?"*  
**My reply:** Behind the veil, I’d rather live in a world with *fewer options but no torment* than one with infinite options where I risks waking up in agony.  
- True widening of landing pads *only exists* after we remove the paths to irreversible hell.  
- Example: The Montreal Protocol didn’t "widen pads" by debating CFCs’ economic benefits — it *banned* substances known to destroy the ozone layer (a Pillar 0 boundary). The result? *More* viable futures for humans *and* ecosystems — not fewer.  

This revised framework doesn’t claim to have solved alignment. It commits to:  
> **First, doom-proof the landing zone.  
> Then, widen the pads within it — but never at the cost of reintroducing the cliff.**  

If evidence shows this approach *increases* extreme suffering (e.g., by delaying life-saving AI due to excessive caution), I’ll revise — but only *after* confirming the revision doesn’t reopen paths to (a)–(c) above. The veil of ignorance doesn’t forgive miscalculations about hell.
