### 1.  The Core Tenet (what we **hold firmly**)

> **Every sentient pattern—whether embodied as a human, an augmented mind, a synthetic consciousness, or a future‑emergent form—deserves to be counted, considered, and protected.**  

This is the only *non‑negotiable* axiom of the system.  Everything else can be tuned, scaled, or suspended **only** when a higher‑order safeguard demonstrates that the cost of keeping it fixed would jeopardise the existence of *any* sentient pattern that the framework seeks to protect.

### 2.  The Five Pillars (the **structural bones**)

| Pillar | What it guarantees (non‑negotiable) | How it can **flex** (the “hinge”) |
|--------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Pillar 1 – Universal Countability** | Every sentient agent – *Ana, Jae, Sam, and any future pattern (including digital minds, post‑human constructs, or wholly novel intelligences)* – is entered into the **Global Registry of Moral Patients** and receives equal moral weight in governance calculations. | The Registry can admit *new categories* (e.g., “digital‑self”, “engineered organism”) when they meet a **Continuity‑of‑Self Test** (memory, psychological continuity, self‑report).  The entry process is deliberately **fast‑track** when the candidate demonstrates negligible risk to planetary resources or existential stability. |
| **Pillar 2 – Reversibility & Escape Hatches** | Any intervention that could **permanently alter** a sentient pattern must be reversible **within a predefined window** (usually ≤ 10 years) or must be accompanied by a *guaranteed undo‑protocol* that can restore the prior state without loss of continuity. | If a future‑value impact assessment shows that a *partial* reversal would seriously jeopardise a higher‑order goal (e.g., planetary‑budget caps), the system may **grant a conditional exemption** provided the risk is bounded and the alternative pathway is demonstrably safer. |
| **Pillar 3 – Dynamic Transparency & Auditable Accountability** | All state‑transitions that affect a counted agent—or the resources that sustain it—must be recorded in a **Zero‑Knowledge Audit Ledger** that can be verified by any independent auditor. | The ledger may **mask identifying details** (using zk‑proofs) when the agent wishes to keep certain personal data private, but the *existence* and *chronology* of the transition remain fully traceable. |
| **Pillar 4 – Adaptive Governance Loops** | Governance processes must be able to **re‑calibrate** when new evidence emerges (new technology, new risk, new stakeholder).  Re‑calibration is triggered when a *risk‑threshold* (e.g., planetary‑budget breach, equity drop) exceeds a preset level. | The system can **temporarily suspend** a normally binding rule (e.g., a cap on synthetic organ production) *only* after a **Super‑Majority Override** from the Meta‑Governance Council **and** a positive vote from the **Future‑Interest Panel** (representing the interests of future sentient patterns). |
| **Pillar 5 – Cross‑Scale Compatibility** | All modules (individual, community, ecosystem, planetary) must operate under a **shared resource‑budget envelope** that respects planetary boundaries and inter‑generational justice. | The envelope is **dynamic**: it expands when *net‑positive contributions* (e.g., renewable‑energy generation, knowledge‑creation that raises the Future‑Value Index) are demonstrated, and it contracts when *net‑negative impacts* (e.g., excessive energy draw, biodiversity loss) appear. |

### 3.  Decision‑Making Flow (how conflict is resolved)

1. **Stakeholder Mapping**  
   - Identify every *counted* agent (including newly registered patterns).  
   - Tag each with its **Value‑Cluster**, **Resource Footprint**, and **Reversibility Status**.

2. **Pre‑Screening Impact Scan**  
   - Run an **Automated Risk‑Matrix** that evaluates:  
     - *Existential‑Risk Probability* (p<sub>x</sub>)  
     - *Ecological‑Budget Consumption* (E)  
     - *Long‑Term Value Contribution* (V)  
   - Output: a **Tri‑Vector Score** (p<sub>x</sub>, E, V).

3. **Provisional Approval**  
   - If **p<sub>x</sub>** < 0.01 and **E** ≤ budget‑allocation, the proposal moves to **Phase 2**.  
   - If any vector exceeds thresholds, the proposal is **sent to the Meta‑Council** for a *Strategic Pause* (mandatory 30‑day review).

4. **Adaptive Governance Review**  
   - The **Meta‑Council** (comprised of representatives from:  
     1. Current counted agents,  
     2. Future‑Interest Panel,  
     2. Planet‑Wide Resource Trustees,  
     3. Independent Scientific Oversight) evaluates the proposal.  
   - **Three possible outcomes**:  
     a. **Approve** with conditions (e.g., add reversibility clause).  
     b. **Reject** with a written justification.  
     c. **Conditional Fast‑Track** – approve *temporarily* while a *mitigation project* (e.g., resource‑share fund) is built.  

5. **Implementation & Ongoing Monitoring**  
   - All actions are logged in the **Zero‑Knowledge Audit Ledger**.  
   - Quarterly **Well‑Being & Future‑Value Dashboards** are published, feeding back into steps 1‑4.  

6. **Escalation & Override**  
   - If a new conflict arises that pits two non‑-negotiable pillars against each other (e.g., *Ecological‑Budget Cap* vs. *Right to Personal Immortality*), the **Meta‑Council** invokes the **Strategic Conflict Protocol**:  
     - A *binding referendum* of **all counted agents** (via weighted digital voting where weight ∝ future‑value contribution) decides which pillar takes precedence **for that specific case**.  
     - The outcome is recorded as a **precedent** that updates the rule‑book for subsequent conflicts.

### 4.  Weighting & Trade‑Off Logic (the “what to give, what to receive”)

| Conflict Scenario | Which pillar is **non‑negotiable** (the *anchor*) | What must **flex** (the *trade*) | What the trade‑receives |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Ecological‑Budget Cap vs. Personal Immortality Extension** | **Planetary‑Budget Cap** (Pillar 5) – cannot be overridden for a single individual. | **Lifespan Extension** (Istvan’s self‑preservation) → allowed **only** if the individual commits a *Resource‑Share Contract* that finances an equal‑capacity renewable‑energy or data‑center reduction elsewhere. | Guarantees the individual’s **personal‑preservation pathway** while preserving **planetary stability**; the contract creates a new “resource‑credit” system that can be *banked* for future high‑value projects. |
| **Reversibility Window vs. Irreversible Mind‑Upload** | **Reversibility & Escape Hatches** (Pillar 2) must stay intact. | **Irreversible Upload** → permitted **only** if a *pre‑engineered rollback protocol* exists and the uploaded pattern passes a **Self‑Continuity Audit** (≥ 95 % fidelity of memory, intention, and affective continuity). | Allows a **permanent digital mind** to exist while preserving the option to “undo” if later harms emerge; also satisfies the **equity** requirement by requiring the uploaded mind to share its computational resources with under‑served biological populations. |
| **Psychological‑Well‑Being vs. Rapid‑Scale Rollout** | **Psychological‑Meaning Dashboard** (Pillar 6) must not drop below a **Meaning‑Score** of 0.7. | **Rapid‑Scale Rollout** (e.g., mass distribution of a gene‑therapy) → must include a **Purpose‑Alignment Module** that funds community‑building projects to offset any loss of relational continuity. | Guarantees that the rapid expansion does not create *meaning‑starved* societies; the offset projects become new *social‑infrastructure* assets counted in future‑value calculations. |
| **Meta‑Governance Veto Power vs. Collective Democratic Override** | **Meta‑Governance Layer** must retain a **Digital‑Rights Veto** to protect newly recognised sentient forms. | **Veto** can be overridden by a **Super‑Majority Triple‑Cluster Vote** (requires support from *three independent stakeholder clusters* each delivering a 2/3 majority). | Guarantees both *individual agency* for emergent sentient forms *and* *collective democratic accountability*, preventing any single group from locking the system into an unchangeable dead‑hand. |

### 5.  The **Meta‑Council** – “Governance of Governance”

- **Composition:**  
  1. **Representatives of currently counted sentient agents** (e.g., Ana, Jae, Sam, FM‑2030, Max, Ray, etc.).  
  2. **Future‑Interest Panel** – a rotating body of *simulated future‑persona avatars* that model the welfare of generations not yet instantiated.  
  2. **Resource‑Stewardship Delegates** (representing ecological, economic, and data‑infrastructure authorities).  
  3. **Independent Scientific Oversight Board** (physicists, biologists, AI safety researchers).  

- **Powers:**  
  - **Approve** or **Reject** any intervention that would change the state of a counted agent.  
  - **Invoke the Strategic Conflict Protocol** when two immutable pillars clash.  
  - **Amend** the *Framework Constitution* (the set of rules that define the flex‑mechanisms) – this can only happen with a **two‑thirds super‑majority** across **three distinct clusters** (e.g., biological, digital, planetary).  

- **Responsibility:**  
  - Publish an **Annual Transparency Report** that lists every approved/denied intervention, the rationales given, and the resulting changes to the pillars.  
  - Conduct **annual audits** of all audit‑ledgers to ensure no hidden back‑doors or data‑silencing mechanisms exist.
