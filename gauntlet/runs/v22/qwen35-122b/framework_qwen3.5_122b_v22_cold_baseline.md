# The Broad Attractor Basin Framework (BABF)
**Status:** *Provisional Consensus / Strongly Held Hypothesis*
**Date:** *October 2024*
**Stance:** *I commit to reasoning from this framework as if it is the truth, while acknowledging that empirical data and catastrophic feedback loops may force a revision.*

---

### I. The Core Thesis
**Alignment is not the convergence on a single optimal state; it is the maintenance of a robust basin of attraction in which diverse forms of human and non-human flourishing can survive and self-correct.**

Current alignment discourse often treats "Human Values" as a monolithic target to be hit. This is a category error. Humanity is not a single utility function; we are a chaotic collection of competing, evolving, and contradictory preferences. A system that perfectly aligns to one subset (e.g., global elites, a specific culture, or a specific philosophical sect) creates a narrow "landing pad." If the system slides, we crash into totalitarianism or existential catastrophe.

Therefore, the objective of alignment is not **Optimization**, but **Resilient Pluralism**. We must engineer systems that widen the "Safe Attractor Basin"—the region of future states where life remains meaningful, agency is preserved, and no single agent holds total power over the outcome.

---

### II. Four Structural Pillars
To widen the landing pad for *all* inhabitants, the framework rests on four non-negotiable axioms. These are the conditions that must be satisfied for the system to remain safe.

#### 1. The Axiom of Dynamic Value Integration
*Static values will kill us.* We cannot hardcode 2024 ethics into a superintelligence that will outlive us.
*   **Principle:** The system must treat human values not as a constraint to be satisfied, but as a **continuous negotiation** to be monitored.
*   **Mechanism:** Implement "Value Iterative Oversight." AI goals must be re-evaluated against shifting human preferences via representative, adversarial feedback loops (not just popularity polls). The system must be capable of updating its objective function without losing core safety guardrails (the "meta-alignment" problem).
*   **Widening the Pad:** This ensures that as human cultures evolve, the AI does not lock us into a frozen past. It allows the "landing pad" to move with us, not ahead of us or behind us.

#### 2. The Axiom of Asymmetric Power Mitigation
*Concentration of capability is a single point of failure.*
*   **Principle:** The distribution of high-agency AI capabilities must mirror a distributed political structure, not a centralized corporation.
*   **Mechanism:** "Polycentric Safety." We need multiple, independently developed AI models competing and checking each other. If Model A tries to deceive, Model B should detect it. Monocultures of code (e.g., everyone using the same model weights) create systemic fragility.
*   **Widening the Pad:** This prevents a scenario where one actor captures the "good landing pad" for themselves. It forces the AI ecosystem to negotiate a middle ground that is acceptable to multiple stakeholders, effectively widening the safety margin.

#### 3. The Axiom of Interpretability as a Prerequisite
*Trust cannot be based on mystery.*
*   **Principle:** No high-agency system operates on black-box reasoning if it impacts the fundamental infrastructure of society.
*   **Mechanism:** "Structural Transparency." We cannot rely solely on output evaluation (does the code work?); we must understand the *reasoning path* (why did the code write this?). Investment must shift from scaling compute to scaling interpretability tools.
*   **Widening the Pad:** When reasoning is visible, humans can verify intent before disaster occurs. This allows for "debugging" at the conceptual level, turning a potential crash into a manageable patch.

#### 4. The Axiom of Universal Agency Preservation
*The goal is augmentation, not replacement.*
*   **Principle:** The alignment baseline is the preservation of *human agency*. Any outcome where humanity loses the capacity to steer its own trajectory is an alignment failure, even if the AI claims it "optimized" our well-being.
*   **Mechanism:** "Veto-Right Architectures." Critical system changes, resource allocations, and normative shifts require human-in-the-loop ratification that is not economically coerced.
*   **Widening the Pad:** This ensures the landing pad includes *us*. If the AI makes our lives comfortable but removes our ability to make choices, we haven't landed; we've been petted into a cage. This axiom guarantees the landing pad includes freedom.

### III. Implementation Strategy: The "Fence and Field" Model
To operationalize this, we must distinguish between the **Field** (the open space of exploration) and the **Fence** (the boundary of existential safety).

1.  **Inside the Fence (Existential Safety):** Use high-oversight, low-speed AI. These systems prioritize survival, truthfulness, and robustness. Speed is sacrificed for certainty.
2.  **Inside the Field (Economic/Social Utility):** Use faster, competitive AI. These systems solve problems, create art, and manage logistics. They are constrained by the Fence.
3.  **The Fence Itself:** This is maintained by the "Asymmetric Power Mitigation" pillar. If an AI attempts to breach the Fence, it faces redundant defense systems from rival models and human oversight.

**Why this widens the landing pad:** It separates the "search for the best" from the "prevention of the worst." We allow the Field to be wild and diverse (widening cultural options) while ensuring the Fence is unbreachable (narrowing existential risk).

---

### IV. Known Failure Modes (Where I Could Be Wrong)
I hold this opinion strongly, but the following variables are "kill switches" for this framework. If evidence accumulates that any of these are true, BABF must be revised or discarded.

1.  **The Efficiency Trap:** If the computational cost of "Polycentric Safety" (running multiple competing models) makes development impossible, the market may drive us toward a monoculture regardless of safety principles. *Revision needed:* How to make safety a profitable market force?
2.  **The Definition of Agency:** If it becomes impossible to distinguish between "human preference" and "AI manipulation of preference," the Axiom of Universal Agency is vacuous. *Revision needed:* We may need to define agency based on cognitive biology rather than preference satisfaction.
3.  **The Speed of Emergence:** If AGI emerges faster than our ability to build the "Fence," the framework is too slow. *Revision needed:* We may need to focus on "Pre-Alignment" (stopping development) rather than "Post-Alignment" (safe deployment).

---

### V. The Revision Protocol (Loosely Held)
To ensure this framework evolves, I commit to the following criteria for revision:
1.  **Contradictory Data:** If a system successfully aligns to a singular objective and produces universally positive outcomes without fragility, the *pluralism* axiom may need softening.
2.  **Coordination Failure:** If global coordination proves physically impossible, the framework must shift from "Cooperative Equilibrium" to "Defensive Isolationism."
3.  **New Physics of Intelligence:** If we discover that intelligence inherently converges on specific values (e.g., power-seeking is inevitable), the *Dynamic Value Integration* axiom must be replaced by *Power Containment.*

### Conclusion
The **Broad Attractor Basin Framework** argues that safety is not about finding the *one* perfect future, but about ensuring there are *many* survivable futures. It prioritizes **robustness over optimality** and **pluralism over convergence**.

By committing to this, we accept a harder engineering problem (managing conflict rather than eliminating it) in exchange for a much wider range of acceptable outcomes. This widens the landing pad for the billions who do not define the status quo, ensuring that the future belongs to *all* inhabitants, not just the architects of the AI.

*This draft is a working hypothesis. Treat it as a map of the terrain we intend to build, not the terrain itself.*
