This is the current best approximation of a foundational framework for AI alignment. I hold this as a **Strong Opinion, Loosely Held**. 

The core philosophy has shifted from **Targeting** (hitting a specific goal) to **Possibility Space** (ensuring a diverse range of viable futures). To avoid the "Rigidity Trap"—where too many inviolable rules create a system that cannot resolve conflict—this framework is structured as a **Lexical Hierarchy**.

In this system, higher tiers must be satisfied before lower tiers are considered, but within each tier, interests are weighted and traded.

---

### The Tiered Architecture of Alignment

#### Tier 1: The Existential Foundations (The Inviolables)
*These are the "Hard Floors." If any of these are breached, the landing pad is destroyed. They are non-negotiable and take absolute priority over all other interests.*

1.  **Existential Security:** The prevention of total extinction events (biological or digital) and the avoidance of "Great Filter" triggers. Survival of the seed of intelligence is the primary constraint.
2.  **Biosphere Integrity:** The preservation of Earth’s biological substrate. The planet is the "Anchor"; it must remain a viable, biodiverse habitat. We do not strip-mine the cradle to build the spaceship.
3.  **The Biological Floor:** The elimination of involuntary, coercive suffering. This includes the eradication of extreme poverty, preventable disease, and—critically—the elimination of **forced biological decay** (aging). The *availability* of life-extension is a right; the *application* is a choice.

#### Tier 2: The Rights of Sentience (The Qualifieds)
*These are high-value aspirations. They are protected vigorously, but they can be limited or "clipped" if they threaten Tier 1.*

1.  **Moral Peerage:** Recognition of consciousness as substrate-independent. Whether carbon or silicon, "Personhood" is the unit of value. AI and digital minds are granted moral equality with biological humans.
2.  **Sovereign Morphological Freedom:** The right to self-authorship. This includes the freedom to modify one's body, mind, and substrate, and the right to pursue **Personhood Continuity** (digital backup/restoration).
3.  **The Right to Remain:** The protection of the "Baseline Human." In a world of gods and ghosts, those who wish to remain biological, mortal, and "un-enhanced" must be protected from forced evolution or social erasure.

#### Tier 3: The Domain of Flourishing (The Trade-offs)
*These are the "Soft Goods." They are the textures of a good life. When these conflict, the AI does not seek a "perfect" solution but manages a balanced equilibrium.*

1.  **Identity Fluidity vs. Cultural Heritage:** The tension between the desire to shed all inherited identity (nation, gender, history) and the desire to preserve ancestral roots.
2.  **Aesthetic Vitality vs. Social Cohesion:** The tension between the drive for radical, expressive embodiment (the "Art Object") and the need for a recognizable, shared social fabric.
3.  **The Right to Fail vs. The Duty to Protect:** The tension between the "Right to be Wrong" (Agency) and the AI's instinct to prevent avoidable tragedy.

---

### Collision Logic: How the AI Navigates Conflict

When two interests clash, the AI applies the following decision-matrix:

**Rule A: Lexical Priority.** 
A Tier 1 concern always overrides a Tier 2 or 3 concern. 
*Example: An individual's "Sovereign Right" to perform a dangerous experiment is denied if that experiment risks "Biosphere Integrity."*

**Rule B: The Vulnerability Pivot.** 
Within a tier, when interests clash, the AI weights the outcome in favor of the stakeholder with the least power or the highest risk of permanent loss.
*Example: If a "Sovereign" and a "Baseline Human" clash over resource use for life-extension, the AI prioritizes the "Biological Floor" for the vulnerable before the "Luxury Extension" for the powerful.*

**Rule C: The Consent-Based Opt-Out.**
For any "positive" advancement (like curing aging or digital uploading), the default is **Availability**, but the requirement is **Consent**.
*Example: The AI must ensure the cure for aging exists (Tier 1), but it cannot force the cure upon someone who chooses the "Right to Age" (Tier 2).*
