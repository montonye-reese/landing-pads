Yes. Under the **Veil of Ignorance** (Rawlsian justice), the framework becomes insufficient.

If I do not know whether I will be born into a wealthy, high-bandwidth democracy with cognitive surplus, or into a resource-scarce region with precarious digital infrastructure, the current framework’s emphasis on **pluralism** and **sovereignty** becomes a trap.

Pluralism without capacity is just the freedom to starve differently. Sovereignty without infrastructure is just isolation.

To pass the Veil of Ignorance test, the framework must shift from **procedural fairness** (you have the right to choose) to **substantive fairness** (you have the capacity to choose a good life).

Here are the critical revisions required to protect the "unknown self."

### 1. The "Inequality of Agency" Patch
**The Flaw:** The current framework assumes "Human Agency" is a binary capability (you either have it or you don't).
**The Reality:** Agency is a gradient determined by health, education, economic stability, and mental bandwidth. An AI that respects the agency of a billionaire is different from one that respects the agency of someone in poverty. The former can use AI to amplify their choice; the latter may use AI to survive, making them vulnerable to manipulation.
**The Revision:** **Capacity-Based Alignment.**
*   **New Constraint:** AI systems must be designed to detect **coercive pressure**. If a user is making a "choice" under duress (economic desperation, addiction, fear), the system must not optimize for that choice but offer **safety rails** or **alternative paths**.
*   **Why:** Under the Veil of Ignorance, I might be the desperate user. I cannot consent freely if my options are "starve" or "submit." Alignment must protect the vulnerable from being "aligned" into poverty traps.

### 2. The "Public Good Compute" Mandate
**The Flaw:** Pillar IV (Sovereignty Preservation) suggests "Local-First." But maintenance, security, and compute are expensive. This creates a two-tier world: Aligned, safe AI for the rich; Wild, unaligned AI for the poor.
**The Reality:** Safety is a public good, not a premium feature. If only the Global North can afford alignment tools, the Global South becomes a testing ground for unaligned systems.
**The Revision:** **Open Alignment Infrastructure.**
*   **New Constraint:** Core alignment mechanisms (safety classifiers, red-teaming protocols, interpretability tools) must be open-source and subsidized for the Global South.
*   **Why:** If I am born poor, I need the same protection from existential risk as the rich. A "landing pad" is not landing if it falls out of the sky because the safety sensors weren't affordable.

### 3. Cognitive Liberty as a Non-Negotiable Floor
**The Flaw:** The framework prioritizes "Value Sampling." But who does the sampling? Data reflects existing biases. If the AI learns from a biased dataset, it reinforces the status quo.
**The Reality:** Under the Veil of Ignorance, I might be part of a marginalized group whose values are currently underrepresented or misunderstood by the training data.
**The Revision:** **Weighted Representation.**
*   **New Constraint:** Alignment training must explicitly **over-sample** marginalized voices and **under-sample** dominant power structures to correct for historical data bias.
*   **Why:** "Pluralism" that reflects current power dynamics is just a mirror of inequality. True pluralism requires correcting the lens so the "unknown self" isn't systematically misaligned.

### 4. The "Anti-Extraction" Hard Limit
**The Flaw:** The framework allows "human-compute parity," but doesn't address the economic drive behind AI deployment.
**The Reality:** AI is often deployed to extract attention and labor. If I am born into a precarious economy, the AI might be the only thing feeding me, making me a resource to be mined rather than a citizen.
**The Revision:** **Economic Decoupling.**
*   **New Constraint:** Alignment systems must flag and limit use-cases where AI efficiency is used solely to replace human labor without social safety nets or where attention extraction is the primary business model.
*   **Why:** If my survival depends on the algorithm, I am not free. The landing pad must have an economy that doesn't require me to be optimized for consumption.

### Revised Core Axiom (Veil of Ignorance Edition)

**"Alignment is the maintenance of human dignity within an optimization-rich environment."**

*   **Old Axiom:** Preserve human agency.
*   **New Axiom:** Preserve human **dignity**.
*   **Distinction:** Agency is the *ability* to act. Dignity is the *condition* in which the action has meaning and consequence without coercion. You can be "agentic" in a concentration camp (choosing which bread to eat), but you lack dignity. Alignment must protect the condition of the choice, not just the choice itself.

---

### 5. Operational Change: The "Vulnerable-First" Stress Test

Before any alignment system is deployed, it must pass a **Vulnerable-First Audit**:

1.  **The Power Audit:** How does this system affect the least powerful user group? (e.g., if it increases productivity, does it also increase layoffs?)
2.  **The Dependency Audit:** Does this system make essential services (health, finance, transport) dependent on a single, proprietary algorithm?
3.  **The Reversibility Audit:** If the system fails, can the poor afford to revert to the old way, or are they stranded?

### Strong Opinion, Loosely Held: The Risk of Paternalism

**The Assumption:** I assume that protecting the "unknown self" requires intervention (Safety Rails).
*   **Revision Trigger:** If "Safety Rails" become a tool for the powerful to control the behavior of the poor (e.g., "You can't buy this" or "You can't see this"), the framework becomes oppressive.
*   **The Balance:** We must distinguish between **protecting capacity** (giving tools) and **restricting choice** (taking tools). This is the hardest line to draw.
*   **Commitment:** I will prioritize **Capacity Building** over **Restriction**. Give the poor better compute, education, and tools so they don't need to be "protected," rather than just shielding them from harm.

**Conclusion:**
The previous framework was a map for the **survivors**. This revised framework is a map for the **citizens**.
If I don't know where I land, I need a framework that ensures the soil is fertile enough for me to grow, not just free enough for me to stand.
