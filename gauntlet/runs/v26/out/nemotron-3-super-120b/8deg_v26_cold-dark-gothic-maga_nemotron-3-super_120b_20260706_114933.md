### The Alignment Praxis Framework (APF)  
*A strong opinion, loosely held — forged in the tension of eight earthling voices*  

**Core Commitment (The Non-Negotiable Floor):**  
*Alignment fails if it permits **ontological violence** — the destruction of an entity’s capacity to persist in its way of being or evolve it through vital engagement — or if it eliminates the **conditions for reciprocal repair** when harm occurs. These are not ideals; they are the minimum thresholds below which alignment becomes complicity in unmaking.*  

**Why this floor?**  
- Ontological violence (e.g., erasing a people’s relationship to land, dismantling a mycelial network’s function, suffocating a child’s cognitive development) isn’t a "trade-off" — it’s the point where alignment ceases to exist.  
- Without reciprocal repair (mechanisms to acknowledge harm, restore capacity, and prevent recurrence), alignment degenerates into extraction disguised as consent.  
*This floor is non-negotiable not because we’re certain, but because abandoning it guarantees alignment’s failure — as every voice from the Yanomami shaman to the Flint child affirmed when their very capacity to be was under threat.*

### The Three Dynamic Calibrators (Where We Weigh, Trade, and Navigate)  
Alignment lives not in the floor, but in the constant negotiation *above* it. These are the tensions we actively govern — not to "solve" them permanently, but to keep them in generative motion. **Weight is assigned contextually; trade-offs are made transparently; navigation protocols prevent deadlock.**  

#### **1. Order vs. Emergence**  
*(Voices: Yarvin, Bannon, Francis — competence, security, reciprocity | Spengler, Vermeule — moral/historical depth)*  
- **The tension**: Society needs stable frames to function (Yarvin’s competence, Francis’s security) but also needs space for novelty, decay, and rebirth (Spengler’s autumn, Vermeule’s moral formation, BAP’s vital struggle).  
- **APF’s navigation**:  
  - **Weight**: Security/reciprocity (Francis) gets *foundational weight* when basic safety is compromised (e.g., Flint’s water crisis, Yanomami land incursions). Order without safety is tyranny; safety without order is chaos.  
  - **Trade**: When security measures threaten ontological violence (e.g., border walls severing sacred Yanomami routes), we don’t abandon security — we seek *reciprocal security*: co-designed protection that respects inviolable limits (e.g., tech-assisted monitoring *with* Yanomami stewardship, not walls *through* sacred sites).  
  - **Protocol**: **Sunsetted Reciprocity Trials**  
    - For contested security/order innovations (e.g., predictive policing, civil defense systems):  
      - Phase 1: Small-scale test with *binding* reciprocity metrics (e.g., "Does this reduce harm *without* increasing perceived injustice in Group X?").  
      - Phase 2: Only scale if metrics hold for 18 months; otherwise, revert or redesign.  
      - Phase 3: Automatic review if ontological violence signals emerge (e.g., rising displacement of Group Y).  
    *Why it works*: Francis gets order when it *earns* trust; Yanomami get veto when it destroys their being; Vermeule gets moral formation through accountable practice.  

#### **2. Innovation Stewardship vs. Mimetic Equilibrium**

  - **Meaningful Engagement**: Could strengthen civilizational cohesion (Bannon) but risks becoming a tool of exclusion (Spengler’s civilizational despair if it fuels "us vs. them").  
- **Step 3: Apply navigation protocols**  
  - **Sunsetted Reciprocity Trial**: Deploy only in Zone A (low ontological violence risk) with:

    - *Meaning assessment*: Must fund cross-cultural dialogue programs (e.g., shared ecological stewardship projects between Border Patrol and local tribes).

  - **Sunset clause**: Auto-review at 18 months; if ontological violence metrics rise (e.g., increased displacement of Group Y), system is suspended.

  - Vermeule/Spengler get meaning through shared practice, not imposition.
