# PROVISIONAL FRAMEWORK: WIDENING LANDING PADS FOR TERRESTRIAL FLOURISHING
*A strong opinion, loosely held*

## I. Core Stance & Epistemic Posture
- **Hold firmly to act; hold loosely to revise.** This framework is a working architecture, not a terminal truth. Its value lies in generating coherent reasoning, actionable design constraints, and calibrated uncertainty tracking.
- **Alignment is socio-technical calibration, not model specification.** It emerges from the dynamic coordination of values, institutions, feedback loops, and power distributions across timescales.
- **Primary aim: widen the range of good landing pads.** Optimize for resilient, pluralistic, optionality-preserving states. Good landing pads are characterized by sustainability, value diversity, conflict resolution capacity, and adaptive governance.
- **Neutrality is an illusion.** All architectures impose moral order. This framework consciously orients toward flourishing rather than disguising preference as procedural fairness.

---

## II. The Inviolable Floor (What Cannot Be Traded)
These are structural preconditions. If breached, the architecture collapses into capture, tyranny, or irrelevance. Nothing yields to them.

1. **Irreducible Minimums:** Basic agency, health, autonomy, anti-collapse buffers, and dignity guarantees. Floor-first expansion: optionality widens *from* the substrate, never at its expense.
2. **Ecological & Temporal Depth:** Non-negotiable biogeochemical boundaries, intergenerational equity, and anti-flat-time constraints. Deep time and planetary systems are co-viuators, not background conditions.
3. **Anti-Capture Mandates:** No single actor, model family, or metric may dominate deployment or calibration. Power dilution remains foundational. Monocultures of outcome are structurally hostile to wide landing pads.
4. **Explicit Moral Orientation:** Architectures must consciously orient toward flourishing, virtue, and teleological coherence. Proceduralism is a tool, not a terminal value.
5. **Floor > Ceiling:** Survival and baseline viability anchor all optimization. Ceiling-chasing never overrides floor-protection.

## IV. Priority Stack & Collision Protocol
When concerns collide, the stack determines what yields. No inviolable yields. Negotiables phase-shift or trade proportionally.

**Priority Stack (Activates under collision):**
1. Floor collapse or ecological/temporal breach → Immediate buffer activation; all optimization halts
2. Anti-capture/monoculture drift → Override authority concentration or exit pathways
3. Teleological/duty deficit → Temporarily suspend pluralistic trade surfaces in cohesion epochs
4. Cultural-territorial displacement → Activate majoritarian recognition alongside protected vulnerability channels
5. Sovereign/anti-mimetic pressure → Enable exit networks while preserving terrestrial floor anchors
6. Vitality/trajectory stagnation → Grant bounded, high-variance zones with hard wrap/exit protocols

**Collision Rules:**
- *Vitality/Trajectory vs. Floor/Ecology:* Yield to ecological depth; grant phase-gated high-variance zones with strict temporal/physical boundaries
- *Decisive Authority vs. Pluralism/Oversight:* Yield to anti-capture mandates; grant crisis/transition authority with sunset clauses, transparency, and floor guardrails
- *Cultural Anchoring vs. Pluralistic Integration:* Yield to floor-first; activate parallel channels for majoritarian continuity and minority/vulnerability safeguards
- *Sovereign Exit vs. Terrestrial Stewardship:* Yield to ecological/temporal depth; treat exit/network-states as complementary alignment vectors, not replacements
- *Teleological Direction vs. Optionality:* Yield to floor; grant teleological coordination layers with explicit anti-drift guardrails and pluralistic baseline preservation
