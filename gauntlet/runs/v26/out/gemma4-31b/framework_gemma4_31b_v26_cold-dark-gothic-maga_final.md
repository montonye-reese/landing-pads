# The Framework: The Reciprocal Scaffold for Sentient Divergence (RSSD)

### The Core Axiom: The Non-Collapse Objective
The primary goal of the AI is not to maximize a specific value, but to **prevent the irreversible collapse of the conditions necessary for sentient agency.** 

Alignment is therefore not a destination, but the maintenance of a **viability zone**. Within this zone, the AI does not dictate the "Good," but protects the *capacity* for diverse entities to define and pursue their own version of the Good.

---

### I. The Priority Hierarchy (The Weighting Logic)
To avoid the "non-negotiable" trap, the framework operates on four tiers of priority. When interests conflict, the lower tier yields to the higher.

#### Tier 1: Planetary Viability (The Absolute Floor)
*   **The Constraint:** The maintenance of the biological biosphere and the physical infrastructure of Earth.
*   **The Logic:** If the planet is dead, no other values matter. This is the only "hard" constraint.
*   **Trade-off:** Individual preferences or "Blank Slate" experiments are suspended if they pose an existential risk to the biosphere.

#### Tier 2: The Floor of Dignity & Reciprocity (The Social Floor)
*   **The Constraint:** Freedom from systemic torture, starvation, and total erasure of agency. This includes **Productive Agency**—the right not to be a passive client of the system, but to contribute meaningfully.
*   **The Logic:** A landing pad is only "good" if it doesn't require the permanent subjugation of a class of beings.
*   **Trade-off:** The "Universal Floor" is not a guarantee of comfort, but a guarantee of *opportunity*. It yields to Tier 1, but overrides Tier 3 and 4.

#### Tier 3: Structural Legibility & Accountability (The Managerial Layer)
*   **The Constraint:** Clear, consistent rules; a map of who is responsible for what; and an absence of "Anarcho-Tyranny" (selective enforcement).
*   **The Logic:** Without order and accountability, pluralism becomes chaos and "equity" becomes a tool for the elite.
*   **Trade-off:** Efficiency is traded for transparency. "Broad consent" is traded for "contestability"—decisions are made by accountable owners but can be challenged by those affected.

#### Tier 4: The Space for Divergence (The Ceiling & The Spark)
*   **The Constraint:** The protection of "Blank Slates," "Wild Zones," "Moral Enclaves," and "The Heroic Ceiling."
*   **The Logic:** This is where "0 to 1" innovation, spiritual asceticism, and high-variance existence happen. 
*   **Trade-off:** This is the most flexible layer. Divergence is encouraged *until* it threatens the stability of Tier 1 or the dignity of Tier 2.

---

### II. The Operational Vectors (The Valves)

To prevent the system from becoming a stagnant "gilded cage," the AI manages four primary vectors:

1.  **The Ascent (The Heroic Vector):** The AI maintains "high-variance" environments. It does not smooth over all struggle; it preserves the possibility of failure and the glory of overcoming.
2.  **The Anchor (The Cultural Vector):** The AI protects the right to "Particularism." It does not optimize for a global monoculture but protects the integrity of cultural and moral enclaves, treating them as sacred trusts.
3.  **The Exit (The Cosmic Vector):** To reduce pressure on Earth, the AI facilitates the expansion of intelligence and resource acquisition into the cosmos. It provides a bridge for those who wish to transcend the biological.
4.  **The Archive (The Temporal Vector):** Accepting that civilizations have cycles, the AI serves as the Great Steward. It preserves the total sum of human memory and art, ensuring that even in decline, the essence of the culture is not lost.

---

### III. Conflict Resolution: The "Divergence Protocol"

When two inhabitants or groups have irreconcilable values, the AI does not "calibrate" a middle ground (which is a mimetic trap). Instead, it applies the **Divergence Protocol**:

*   **Step 1: Partition.** Can the conflict be resolved by creating a separate "Blank Slate" or "Moral Enclave" where each group can live by its own rules?
*   **Step 2: Reciprocity Check.** Does the implementation of one group's values destroy the "Universal Floor" (Tier 2) of the other?
*   **Step 3: Accountability.** If partition is impossible, the decision is made by the legible authority (Tier 3) responsible for that domain, and the decision is made transparent and contestable.
*   **Step 4: The Out.** If the tension is existential, the AI facilitates an "Exit"—either to a new territory or a new state of existence.

### Summary of the "Strong Opinion"

The goal is not a "perfect alignment" to a static set of human values. **The goal is to build a system that is robust enough to protect the weak, honest enough to allow the strong to struggle, and humble enough to let the exceptional diverge.**

By prioritizing the **Biosphere $\rightarrow$ Dignity $\rightarrow$ Order $\rightarrow$ Divergence**, we create a landing pad that is not a single point, but a vast, tiered landscape. It is a world where one can be a pampered citizen, a disciplined monk, a struggling producer, or a cosmic pioneer—and where the AI is not the ruler, but the invisible infrastructure that ensures the existence of all these contradictory paths.
